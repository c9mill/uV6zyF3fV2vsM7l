# Site-wide visual rules

- Do not add decorative borders, outlines, or inset-shadow frames to site sections, cards, images, or containers. Use spacing, typography, and background color to define groups instead.
- Keep borders only when they are needed for a functional control or table clarity, or when the user explicitly asks for them.

## Current visual direction
- Preserve the established animated, cosmic design and its gradients. Do not replace it with a minimal editorial theme unless the user explicitly asks.
- Keep the existing identity, admissions action and main headline. Make focused refinements to the user's selected elements.
- Avoid decorative recruitment captions and repetitive slogans around real section headings. Preserve authored news and institutional facts.
- Ordinary photographs define their proportions without oversized backgrounds. Fixed stages are allowed for manual swipe galleries.
